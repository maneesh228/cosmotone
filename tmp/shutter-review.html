Automatic Rolling Shutters from Thrissur, Supplied Across Kerala

Krishna Shutters is based in Thrissur and supplies automatic rolling shutters across Kerala. Contact us to discuss shutter requirements for your shop, warehouse, garage or other property, including motorized and remote controlled options.

When choosing a shutter, consider the opening dimensions, how frequently it will be used, the material and the required controls. Ask our team about the available specifications, power-failure operation, warranty and delivery arrangements for your location..

To request a quotation, share your town or district, the approximate width and height of the opening, and a photograph of the site. Mention whether you need a new shutter or want to discuss automation options for an existing shutter.
